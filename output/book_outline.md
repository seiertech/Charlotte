# Book Proposition
You are not a motivation problem. Not a willpower problem. Not a knowledge problem. You are a **system** — layered, connected, running whether you look at it or not. Biological hardware, running a mostly-hidden operating system, executing habits and skills, connected to other people, all organised around a story you tell about who you are. Everything in you runs on cause and effect. Nothing you do is random. Every effect has a cause, and every cause has a *location* in the system. Because of that, anything can be traced — and anything traced can be changed. Find where a problem lives, work at the right layer, in the right order, and it moves.

# Target Reader
Anyone who has ever felt:
- exhausted but can’t switch off
- stuck in habits they can’t break
- lonely even when surrounded by people
- like they’re working against themselves
- like they’ve been handed a life they didn’t get the manual for

Anyone who has ever thought: *I know what I should do… I just can’t do it.*
