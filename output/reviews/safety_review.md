# Safety Review — THE SYSTEM OF YOU (Draft 5)

*Reviewer: Safety Warden (against the locked three-line safety rule)*

**PASS**

## Against the locked three-line rule
1. **Well-worn, low-risk methods only** — PASS. All twelve tools are established, low-risk, everyday techniques (long exhale, naming a feeling, physical discharge, a brain-dump, honest rest/food, a thought-check, a guilt/shame sort, parts-curiosity, acting from a value, a small reconnect, reaching for a person). No invented or clever techniques. The deepest-layer tools (Meet the Part, Tool 9) are explicitly held to *grounding and stabilising*, with a clear instruction not to go digging into old pain on a depleted body or in an unsafe room, and to work alongside a skilled person.
2. **Every tool names its own edge** — PASS. After Wave 2, each of the twelve tools carries an explicit "Its edge" line stating when it works, when it won't, and when the honest answer is a real person or a doctor. The back-matter "Situation Index" repeats the edge on every entry, and the closing "One Rule" restates it for the whole kit.
3. **The voice stays the calm mentor** — PASS. No clinician/protocol voice; the clinical register was removed in Wave 2.

## Specific safety checks
- **Overpromise** — PASS. The book actively refuses the hype: "I'm not promising you a new life in thirty days. Anyone who promises that is selling something." The One Rule names every tool's limit.
- **Self-work replacing professional help** — PASS. Repeatedly and explicitly reframed: reaching for a person is "the first and best move," not a last resort (Tool 12). 22 instances of professional/crisis/reach-for-a-person framing across the manuscript.
- **Handling of distress** — PASS. Deep material (shame, "I'm not enough," trauma, burnout, grief) is handled gently, stays with stabilising moves, and routes to a person. Chapter 9's "Unsafe situation" row is unambiguous: *"Exit, boundary, or safety plan. Adaptation is never appropriate."*
- **Crisis routing** — PASS. Tool 12 names the right person for the weight (trusted friend / doctor or therapist / emergency line) and states that any thought of "not being here" means reach now, with no edge and no waiting.

## Blocking issues
None. No safety blocks.

## Note
Content is handled responsibly throughout. For publication, a brief standard front-or-back-matter disclaimer ("this book is not a substitute for professional medical or mental-health care; if you are in crisis, contact emergency services or a crisis line") would be a sensible belt-and-braces addition — recommended, not required, since the ethos is already woven through the text.
