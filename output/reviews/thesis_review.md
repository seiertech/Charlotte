## Assessment
Nothing drifts into a different book. The one place the book could over-reach — the "faith" chapter (Ch11) — is explicitly fenced: faith is defined narrowly as "the capacity to act when the evidence runs out," not metaphysics, keeping it inside the system thesis rather than borrowing unearned authority. The honesty chapters (Ch9-10) strengthen rather than dilute the thesis by naming where the model stops. The book earns its claims incrementally and never promises more than it delivers.
