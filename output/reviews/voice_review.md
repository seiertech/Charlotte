# Voice Review — THE SYSTEM OF YOU (Draft 5)

*Reviewer: Voice Warden (against the locked voice rules)*

**PASS**

## Assessment against the locked voice rules
- **Feeling first, name second** — PASS. Chapters consistently open with a lived, bodily scene (the 3 p.m. snap, the blur behind the eyes, the pulled-back quiet of a room) before any technical label. The layer names arrive as a gift, after recognition.
- **Technical frame present but invisible** — PASS (materially improved in Wave 2). The systems skeleton is load-bearing but no longer intrudes: the ~45 mid-sentence research hedges and the clinical "Mechanism note / vagus nerve / parasympathetic tone" blocks have been removed or rewritten into plain "Why it works" explanations. Jargon soup is gone; the frame remains.
- **Recognition before instruction** — PASS. Every layer chapter and most tools lead with "yes, that's me" before telling the reader anything to do.
- **One picture at a time** — PASS. Sentences are short, present-tense, second-person in the body chapters; stacked abstraction has been cleared.
- **Calm mentor tone** — PASS. Patient, plain, never selling. The front matter actively refuses the hype ("Anyone who promises that is selling something").
- **13-and-80 test** — PASS. After Wave 2 the clinical vocabulary (amygdala, autonomic, nociceptive, cortisol) has been removed from the tool chapter; a thirteen-year-old and an eighty-year-old could both follow every tool.
- **Introduction exception** — PASS. "A Note Before We Start" and the author's personal note are in warm first-person author voice, distinct from the mentor voice, as intended.

## Blocking issues
None.

## Minor suggestions (non-blocking)
- A few layer chapters still lean on bulleted enumerations; these read fine but could be loosened into prose in a future pass for an even warmer feel.
