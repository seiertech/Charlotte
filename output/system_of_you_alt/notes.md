# Notes

The ideas in this book draw on research in sleep science, stress physiology, and cognitive and behavioural psychology. These notes point to sources for the specific claims made in the text, for readers who want to go further. You never need them to use the book.

Two honest cautions. First, a single study is a starting point, not a final word; where a finding comes from one small experiment, it is offered as illustration, not proof. Second, some well-known ideas in popular psychology, including "willpower depletion" and certain "priming" effects, have not held up well when other researchers tried to repeat them, so this book does not rely on them.

2. Yoo, S.-S., Gujar, N., Hu, P., Jolesz, F. A., & Walker, M. P. (2007). The human emotional brain without sleep: a prefrontal amygdala disconnect. *Current Biology*, 17(20), R877–R878.
