# THE SYSTEM OF YOU: Alt Edition

An alternative edition of *The System of You*, written to compete as a mid-list to bestselling self-help book. The original edition (`output/full_fifth_draft.md`) is untouched; this folder is a separate, parallel version.

Source: `output/full_fifth_draft.md` on main at commit `b3732b4` (13 October 2026), plus `foundation/character_bible.md` and `foundation/model_bible.md`.

## The brief (from the author)

- Keep the message, the methodology and the system exactly.
- Keep the author's voice, and keep the author's note and dedication as close to the original as possible.
- Make everything else "fire": a book people finish, use and pass on.
- The book is global, not localised: no references to specific helplines or crisis numbers.
- Run the dedication at the end of the book.

## What stays exactly as it was

- **The five layers:** Body, Wiring, Habit, Room, Story, in that order.
- **The three costs:** Charge, Fuel, Pressure. No fourth currency (the draft's toolkit had drifted into a "Mind" currency; that is removed).
- **The one rule:** you can't reliably fix a higher layer while a lower one is broken. Start at the bottom.
- **The two questions:** Which layer? What is it costing me?
- **The twelve tools,** with their names, their steps and their honest edges.
- **The five kinds of constraint, Belief then Faith, and the Growth Map.**
- **The practice structure:** Notice, Trace, Try, Watch, Adjust, and the "one line for the picture of yourself" that builds the Growth Map.
- **The cast:** Maren, Callum, Grace, Marcus and Elena, held to the character bible.
- **The dedication,** word for word.
- **The author's personal story** in "A Note Before We Start," kept in the author's words with light proofreading only.

## What changes, and why

| Change | Why it should sell better |
|---|---|
| One repeatable idea: **find the layer** | Hits are built on a phrase readers repeat to a friend ("let them", "tiny changes"). |
| The reframe lands on page one | "You are not a mess. You are a system" is the relief moment; it now opens the book instead of arriving after setup. |
| Each layer chapter is built around one character's real moment | Stories carry the ideas; the draft read like a specification. |
| Caveats are said once, clearly, then trusted | The draft repeated "hard cases / counterexamples / model limitations" in every chapter, which kept cancelling the warmth. |
| A same-day win in chapter one | Readers who get relief early trust the rest of the book. |
| Shorter, denser, about 50,000 words | The common criticism of hits is padding. Word of mouth favours books that don't waste the reader's time. |
| Chapter endings worth sharing | Quotable lines do much of a self-help book's marketing for free. |
| Character continuity fixed | The draft had a widowed Marcus discussing dishes with a partner, Callum as a former marathon runner, and Grace as an artist posting online. All five now match the character bible. |
| Dedication moved to the end | At the author's request; it now reads as the book's final words. |

## Assumptions made while the author was away

1. **Dedication placement:** after the last chapter and before the quick-reference toolkit and notes, so it is the last thing a reader reads in the narrative.
2. **First-person stories:** only the author's stated facts are used (the book began while he was undergoing hospital tests; he wrote it "from the middle of it"). Draft anecdotes that may not be true for the author are not repeated.
3. **Crisis guidance:** wording that works in any country ("your local emergency number", "a doctor", "someone near you") instead of named services.
4. **Spelling:** British English, matching the original.
5. **Notes:** a smaller set, limited to claims the text actually makes, with contested research flagged plainly.
