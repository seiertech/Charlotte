# Marketing Pack: The System of You (Alt Edition)

Draft copy for the cover, the back of the book and online stores. All of it is written to match the manuscript: no claims the book doesn't make, and no named helplines.

---

## Title

**Recommended: keep the title.**

> **THE SYSTEM OF YOU**
> *The Manual You Were Never Given*

It's already strong: one clear concept, an emotional promise, and the cover artwork is built around it. Changing it would throw away work that's done.

### Alternative subtitles, if you want to test them

1. *The Manual You Were Never Given* (current; warm, curious, broad)
2. *Find the Layer. Fix the Right Thing. Stop Fighting Yourself.* (most practical; mirrors the method)
3. *Why You Snap, Stall and Struggle, and Where to Start Instead* (most problem-led; strongest for online search)
4. *A Manual for the Most Complicated Thing You'll Ever Run* (most playful)

If you only test one, test option 3 against the current subtitle in a small ad campaign. Problem-led subtitles often win online, while the current one tends to work better face-out in a bookshop.

## Online store description (about 300 words)

> **You are not a mess. You are not a mystery. You are a system.**
>
> It's three in the afternoon. Something small lands, a message, a question, one request too many, and you snap. Then comes the question that has followed you for years: *why did I just do that?*
>
> Most of us answer the same way: *something is wrong with me.* *The System of You* shows you why that answer is almost always wrong, and what to do instead.
>
> Every person runs on five layers:
>
> - **Body**: the machine you run on
> - **Wiring**: the part of you that reacts before you decide
> - **Habit**: the paths you've worn by repetition
> - **Room**: the people and places that set your weather
> - **Story**: the sentence you tell yourself about who you are
>
> When something goes wrong, the cause lives in one of them. Work on the wrong one, and nothing holds. Find the right one, and change finally becomes possible.
>
> **Inside, you'll discover:**
>
> - Why willpower fails, and the one rule that explains it
> - The two questions that locate almost any problem in under two minutes
> - The three hidden costs, Fuel, Pressure and Charge, that drive how you feel
> - Twelve plain, practical tools for your hardest days, each honest about what it can and can't do
> - How to keep going when understanding isn't enough
> - A simple way to see, in your own handwriting, how far you've come
>
> Told through five people you'll recognise, a nurse running on empty, a student whose words fire too fast, a manager stuck in her own reliability, a widower who won't say how he's doing, and a deputy head waiting to be found out, this is a warm, practical and deeply human guide to understanding yourself at last.
>
> **For anyone ready to stop fighting themselves, and start working with themselves.**

## Launch notes

- **Timing:** self-help sales climb in December and peak in January. A release in late autumn gives early reviews time to build before the New Year rush.
- **Share-ready content:** each chapter ends with a line worth sharing, and the five characters' stories work well as short text-on-screen posts. The Chapter 1 "five people, one snap" sequence is the strongest single piece for social media.
- **Lead magnet:** the one-page method ("How it works, in one page", from the author's note) plus the Toolkit at a glance works well as a free printable in exchange for an email address.
- **Reviews:** a handful of honest early readers from the target audience, before launch, will matter more than any single marketing channel.
