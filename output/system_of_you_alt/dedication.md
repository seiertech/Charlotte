# Dedication

*To My Person, thank you for helping me start believing in dreams again.*

*There was a time when I put my dreams away. Life was busy, time was passing, and it felt easier to forget them than to keep carrying them.*

*Then I learned something profound from you, who always showed me unconditional love.*

*You never stopped dreaming and believing in yourself. To you life is an adventure. Experiences are gifts. You have proven to me that love is enough. And you made me believe again.*

*This book was born in that believing.*

*Not just a collection of pages. Proof that together we can still do anything, it is never too late.*

*Never too late to start again, to love, to remember, and to dream a new dream.*

*My wish for you is simple: Never stop dreaming and never stop believing. Because the moment you do, everything changes.*

*My love and gratitude began before memory, as we shared the same womb, from where our lives began, and from where our dreams first took root together.*

*For you My Person, A Dreamer from an early age, keep on dreaming.*
