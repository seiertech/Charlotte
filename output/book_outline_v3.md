### **Book Proposition** *(locked core thesis, restated faithfully)*
You are not a motivation problem, a willpower problem, or a knowledge problem. You are a **system**—layered, connected, and running on cause and effect. Every effect has a cause, and every cause has a *location* in the system. Find where a problem lives, work at the right layer in the right order, and it moves. That is the whole promise.

### **Target Reader**
- Anyone who has ever felt stuck, exhausted, or like they’re fighting themselves—without understanding why.
- Readers who want **practical tools** (not just theory) to diagnose and change what’s not working.
- People who are tired of vague self-help and want a **clear, step-by-step manual** for how they actually work.
- Age range: 13 to 80. No prior knowledge required—just the ability to recognize tiredness, tension, or frustration.

### **Chapter Plan**

#### **Chapter 0: Introduction** *(author’s first-person voice)*
**Title:** *A Note Before We Start*
**One idea:** This book is the manual you were never given for how you actually work—and it starts with relief, not effort.
**Builds on:** The author’s note (true, unpolished story of where this came from and who it’s helped).
**Must NOT reveal yet:**

#### **Chapter 1: The Doorway** *(the two anchors + five-cause proof)*
**Title:** *You Are a System*
**One idea:** The same symptom (e.g., a 3pm snap) can have five different causes—and the cause determines what fixes it.
**Builds on:** The Introduction’s promise of relief through understanding.
**Must NOT reveal yet:**
- The names of the five layers (introduce them as "five places it could be coming from").
- The currencies (charge/pressure/fuel) as a framework.
- The mechanism of how layers constrain each other.
**Required practice/example:**
- **The ONE full scene:** Five people experiencing the same symptom (e.g., snapping at their kid at 3pm), each with a **different physical signature** (e.g., jaw clenching, hands shaking, stomach sinking, spine stiffening, inner sentence looping).
- *"Notice this in you"*: *"Which one was yours? Not the symptom—the cause. Where did it live in your body?"*
**Commonly mistaken for:**
- A willpower problem (for all five).

#### **Chapter 2: Body**
**Title:** *The Thing That Holds You Up*
**One idea:** Your body is not the stage for your life—it’s the foundation. When it’s broken, nothing above it works right.
**Builds on:** The Doorway’s "one symptom, five causes" (the body as one of them).
**Must NOT reveal yet:**
- Wiring/Habit/Room/Story layers.
- The sequencing rule ("work from the bottom up").
- The currencies beyond *"what is it costing you?"* (e.g., fatigue = fuel; tension = charge).
**Required practice/example:**
- **Micro-scene:** Someone ignoring exhaustion until their body "goes on strike" (e.g., falling asleep mid-sentence).
- *"Notice this in you"*: *"Where in your body do you feel the weight of being tired? Not sleepy—tired. Heavy. Slow."*
**Commonly mistaken for:**
- A motivation problem (e.g., *"I’m just lazy"*).
**Sensory grammar:** Weight, thinness, blur, temperature.
