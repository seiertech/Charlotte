# Editorial Rewrite Strategy — THE SYSTEM OF YOU (Draft 2 → Draft 3)

*Response to the first full editorial critique. This is a strategy, not a rewrite. Nothing is regenerated until the gating decisions below are made.*

---

## 0. Governing rules for the whole rewrite

1. **Originality & attribution (non-negotiable).** The comparables (Clear, Burkeman, Julie Smith, van der Kolk, Maté) are studied for *technique only*. No sentence, structure, or example is reproduced. Any idea traceable to a specific author or work is named in-text as theirs ("as van der Kolk puts it…") — never absorbed as ours. Mechanism/science content uses well-established, general principles; where a specific claim needs backing, it is attributed to a real, checkable source, and we do **not** invent citations. Originality lives in the *layering* and the *voice*, not in borrowed frameworks.
2. **The foundation is currently "locked" — this critique asks us to unlock it.** Several of the strongest recommendations (rename the layers, demote the currencies, move Belief/Faith to the finale, appendix the tables) directly revise `foundation/Me_OS_Foundation.md` and its reveal order. That file is the source of truth the whole pipeline obeys. **We do not touch it silently.** Step 1 of this strategy is a deliberate, author-approved revision of the foundation to **v2**. Everything downstream regenerates from that.
3. **Sequence is law.** We follow the critique's order of operations. The human-only steps (author's note, "what this book is not," the spine decision) come first because everything else stands on them.

---

## 1. The diagnosis — accepted

**The book's thesis and its body contradict each other.** The thesis says the same symptom comes from five different layers and knowing which is everything. The body gives every person on every layer the same tight chest, clenched shoulders, and *"why can't I just—"*. The prose teaches the reader that the layers are interchangeable, which is the opposite of the argument. This is correct, and it is the root cause. Fix this one thing and half the other problems dissolve.

The single highest-value move in the entire critique is the **five-people / one-symptom / five-causes rebuild** (Part 3). It *demonstrates* the thesis instead of asserting it. It becomes Chapter 1's proof-of-concept and the recurring cast for the whole book. We build the rewrite around it.

## 2. The gating decisions (author's call — everything waits on these)

These four require your ruling because they change the *locked* foundation. My recommendation is given, with the reasoning.

### Decision A — The spine metaphor: rename the layers?
**Critique:** the computer metaphor is 4/5 consistent (Identity isn't a computing term), underused (the taxonomy evaporates after the chapter titles), and a tired genre cliché. Rename to plain English: **Body / Wiring / Habit / Room / Story.**

**My recommendation: YES — rename to plain English, and here's the clean reconciliation with the "locked" foundation.** Your foundation's *own* deepest voice rule is **"feeling first, name second… plain words out of confidence."** Applying that rule to the taxonomy itself *demands* the plain names be primary. So this isn't breaking the foundation — it's the foundation finally obeying itself. Keep the computing words as the *optional* "technical name second" (Body — the hardware you run on), used sparingly, then retired. This also fixes the Identity inconsistency at a stroke.
→ **Proposed names: Body / Wiring / Habit / Room / Story** (with Body/Wiring/Habit/Room/Story as headers; computing terms demoted to a single gloss each).

### Decision B — The currencies (Charge / Fuel / Pressure)
**Critique:** demote from a parallel framework to a single diagnostic tool inside Body/Wiring.

**My recommendation: PARTIAL — demote, but keep the two-question method.** The foundation's core method is *"which layer, which currency."* The currencies shouldn't be a standalone Part with their own chapter (that's the "second framework" bloat), but they must survive as the **"what is it costing you"** axis of the diagnostic — which is exactly what the Troubleshooting Reference's "Costing you" column already is. So: **cut the standalone currencies chapter; fold currencies into the diagnostic method and the reference as the cost-axis.** This keeps the reference coherent and removes a rival framework. Clean.

### Decision C — Belief / Faith (the "Meaning" material)
**Critique:** it's a chapter from a more interesting book about volition; move it to the **finale** where it answers the question every reader has on the last page — *"I understand, and I still won't do it. Why?"*

**My recommendation: YES.** This is a genuinely great structural insight. The foundation already places Meaning late; we move it to the *true* end, after the method/tools/worked-problems, reframed explicitly as the answer to volition. That becomes the book's emotional and intellectual climax. (Note: the critique calls it "Belief→Faith→Works" — the foundation is "Belief, then Faith." We keep the foundation's two-part version unless you want to add "Works"/volition as the third beat, which actually fits the finale framing well.)

### Decision D — The reference tables (Troubleshooting + Growth Map/Boundary)
**Critique:** reference material, not chapters — make them an appendix.

**My recommendation: YES (already half-done).** The Troubleshooting Reference is already an appendix. The Boundary currently has a full chapter (ch08) and the Growth Map is the finale (ch15) — we keep the Growth Map as narrative finale material but move its *tabular* parts to the appendix, and fold the Boundary's teachable content into the relevant layers while its table joins the reference.

## 3. The rewrite plan (phased — mirrors the critique's order of operations)

### Phase 0 — Human prerequisites (only you can do these)
- **P0.1 The author's note.** The book's entire pitch is "I'm not selling you anything, I'll tell you the truth," and its one moment of testimony is a placeholder. Nothing else matters until this is real. **You write it** — short, true, even badly. I cannot and will not fabricate it.
- **P0.2 "What this book is not."** Two frontmatter pages distinguishing a system running low from depression, anxiety, trauma, and chronic illness — and saying plainly when the move is a doctor. **I will draft this for your approval** (you sign off on the clinical boundaries).

### Phase 1 — Revise the foundation to v2 (from Decisions A–D)
Produce `foundation/Me_OS_Foundation_v2.md`: renamed spine, currencies demoted to a tool, Belief/Faith relocated to the finale, tables designated appendix, and a **revised reveal order** reflecting all of it. This is the new locked source of truth. Everything regenerates from here.

### Phase 2 — Build the cast (the structural core)
Rebuild the five people: **same external symptom (they snap at someone at 3pm), five different causes, one per layer.** Priya = Body (five hours' sleep). Jordan = Wiring (reaction before decision). Maya = Habit (worn groove). Alex = Room (someone else's weather). Fifth person = Story ("I'm the one who holds it together, so I'm the one allowed to break"). This is Chapter 1's six-page proof-of-concept, and **all five recur in every chapter**, changing at different rates — **and one of them fails** (the honesty that earns trust).

### Phase 3 — Sensory grammar (the prose discipline that fixes the contradiction)
Assign each layer its own physical register and **enforce it at the agent level**:
| Layer | Register | Forbidden |
|---|---|---|
| Body | weight, thinness, blur, temperature | anything psychological |
| Wiring | speed, gear-slip, arrival-before-decision | anything slow |
| Habit | groove, automaticity, sleepwalking | anything effortful |
| Room | temperature, volume, pull, weather | anything solitary |
| Story | narration, the uninvited sentence, the coat | anything sensory |

**Ban outright:** *chest, clench, shoulders, lands like a stone, why can't I just—, the words swim.* One may be earned back per chapter only by writing something better first. Give each of the five people a **different body**: Priya's jaw/voice, Jordan's hands/eyes, Maya's stomach, Alex's room-temperature, the fifth's narration. **Nobody else gets a chest.**

### Phase 4 — Scene rationing
**One** full scene in the whole book — in the Doorway — the best 500 words in the manuscript. Everywhere else: **micro-scenes**, one to three sentences, dropped as evidence mid-argument, never as overture. Cut all nineteen scene-openings and the pasted duplicates.

### Phase 5 — Point of view
Break the ~95% "you." **"You"** for diagnostic passages; genuine **third person** for the five case studies; **"I"** for the argument. The absence of "I" in a book about self-knowledge is the strange omission the critique nails — and it's blocked by the missing author's note (Phase 0), which is why that comes first.

### Phase 6 — Write the missing 25,000–35,000 words (the real intellectual content)
Target 55k–70k total. The new words are **not padding** — they're the three things currently absent:
- **Mechanism (~10k):** *why* lower layers constrain higher ones; what actually fails at the boundaries; why Story-work can't fix a Body problem. This is the intellectual core. Attribution rules (§0.1) apply hardest here.
- **Failure modes:** every layer gets a **"commonly mistaken for"** section (e.g., a Room problem misdiagnosed as a Story problem = years of therapy about your father when the real issue is your job).
- **The hard cases:** the reader who diagnosed correctly and still won't move; the reader whose lowest layer *isn't* fixable (chronic illness, poverty, a marriage they can't leave). The book currently assumes the lowest layer is always adjustable; the reader knows that's untrue. Facing it honestly earns more trust than the rest of the book combined.

### Phase 7 — Protect the good
Do not let the rewrite kill these. They are the seeds to build outward from:
- *"You're not sleepy. You're thinned out."* (→ epigraph of the Body chapter)
- *"feet finding the rug in the dark with the precision of a blind man reading Braille"* (proof a layer can have its own texture)
- *"I'm the kind of person who always puts others first."* (Identity becoming visible)
- *"the coffee maker starts its familiar hiss before you've even decided to get up"* (habit shown, not told)
- *"Reaching out isn't the last resort. It's the first tool you were ever given."* (the closer)
- *"I'm not promising you a new life in thirty days. Anyone who promises that is selling something."* (the positioning)

### Phase 8 — Read aloud
Final pass. Every scene that skims, goes.

## 4. How the machine changes to execute this

The lean conveyor stays, but the agents are re-tuned to enforce the new rules:
- **New foundation v2** becomes the context every agent reads.
- **Chapter Architect** gets the five-cast continuity map + each chapter's assigned layer, sensory register, and "commonly mistaken for" slot.
- **Drafter** gets: the per-layer sensory grammar, the banned-vocabulary list (hard fail), the POV rule (you/third/I by passage type), scene-rationing (micro-scenes only outside the Doorway), and the differentiated-body rule for the five people.
- **New agent — Continuity/Cast Warden:** checks that the five people stay distinct (different bodies, different causes) and that banned vocabulary hasn't crept back. This is the guardrail that stops the thesis-vs-body contradiction from returning.
- **New agent — Mechanism/Attribution reviewer:** for the new mechanism sections — flags any unsupported claim and any phrasing that echoes a source too closely (originality guard).
- **Voice Warden** updated to enforce the sensory-grammar bans and the POV alternation.
