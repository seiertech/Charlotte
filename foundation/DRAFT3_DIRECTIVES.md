# DRAFT 3 DIRECTIVES — operational spec for the rewrite

*This sits on top of `Me_OS_Foundation.md`. Where it conflicts with the original foundation's REVEAL ORDER or naming, THIS DOCUMENT WINS. It encodes the locked editorial decisions (A–E) from EDITORIAL_REWRITE_STRATEGY.md. Every agent must read this.*

## Model
- **Prose workhorse:** `mistralai/mistral-large-3-675b-instruct-2512` (proven, fast enough).
- DeepSeek-V4-Pro produces marginally stronger differentiation but is ~10× slower (impractical for a full book). Reserve it, if ever, for a "hero pass" on the Doorway + Chapter 1 proof only.

## Decision A — Rename the layers (plain names primary, computing term optional gloss)
| Old (computing) | NEW primary name |
|---|---|
| Hardware | **Body** |
| Operating System | **Wiring** |
| Software | **Habit** |
| Network | **Room** |
| Identity | **Story** |
Use the plain name always; the computing term may appear once as a gentle gloss, then retire. This obeys the foundation's own rule: *feeling first, name second; plain words.*

## Decision B — Currencies demoted
Charge / Fuel / Pressure are NOT a standalone framework or chapter. They survive only as the **"what is it costing you"** axis of the diagnostic (the *which layer, which currency* method) and as the "Costing you" column of the Troubleshooting Reference. Never give them their own reveal-order beat.

## Decision C — Belief/Faith → the finale
The Meaning material (belief, then faith) moves to the **end** of the book, reframed as the answer to the reader's real last-page question: *"I understand — and I still won't do it. Why?"* It is the volition/why-we-don't-act climax, not a mid-book chapter.

## Decision D — Tables → appendix
Troubleshooting Reference and any Growth-Map/Boundary grids live in an appendix, not as chapters.

## Decision E — Differentiated third person for the taxonomy; "you" for the turn
- The thesis is a **taxonomy** (one symptom, five causes). A taxonomy needs **comparison**, which needs several subjects side by side — so the proof is **third person**: five distinct people, one shared symptom, five causes, each with a **genuinely different body**.
- Then pivot to **second person** for the turn: *"Which one was yours?"*
- The reader's own arc runs through the "Notice this in you" practices → Growth Map (that's their self-portrait). No named recurring cast required.

## POV — division of labour
**Third person demonstrates. Second person applies. First person ("I") argues.** (The "I" passages need the author's note to exist first — see prerequisites.)

## Sensory grammar (enforced per layer — makes the causes *feel* different)
| Layer | Register | Forbidden |
|---|---|---|
| Body | weight, thinness, blur, temperature | anything psychological |
| Wiring | speed, gear-slip, reaction-before-decision | anything slow |
| Habit | groove, automaticity, sleepwalking | anything effortful |
| Room | temperature, volume, pull, weather | anything solitary |
| Story | narration, the uninvited sentence, the coat | anything sensory |

## Banned vocabulary (hard fail — earn one back per chapter only by writing something better first)
`chest`, `clench`, `clenched`, `shoulders`, `lands like a stone`, `why can't I just`, `the words swim`. Give each person a **different** physical signature (e.g. jaw/voice, hands/eyes, stomach, neck/room-temperature, spine/inner-sentence).

## Scene rationing (the deepest change: overture → evidence)
**One** full scene in the whole book — in the Doorway — the best 500 words in the manuscript. Everywhere else: **micro-scenes** of 1–3 sentences dropped as *evidence mid-argument*, never as overture.

## Length
Target 55k–70k total. The new words are **mechanism** (why lower layers constrain higher ones), **failure modes** (each layer's "commonly mistaken for"), and **hard cases** (the lowest layer that won't move). Not padding.

## Originality & attribution (non-negotiable)
Comparables (Clear, Burkeman, Julie Smith, van der Kolk, Maté) are studied for technique only — nothing reproduced. Any idea traceable to a specific author is named as theirs in-text. Mechanism claims use general, established principles with real attribution; never invent citations; never lift phrasing.

## The NEW reveal order (supersedes the foundation's)
0. **Introduction** — author's first-person voice (needs the author's note).
1. **The Doorway** — the two anchors (you are a system; cause and effect) + the ONE full scene + the **five-cause proof** (five people, one 3pm snap, five causes/bodies) → *"Which one was yours?"*
2. **Body** · 3. **Wiring** · 4. **Habit** · 5. **Room** · 6. **Story** — each: recognition → mechanism → "commonly mistaken for" → a "Notice this in you" practice. Currencies appear only as the cost-axis.
3. (mid) **The method made whole** — *which layer, which currency*; the sequencing rule; the self-check.
4. **The Toolkit** (clean reference) → **worked situations** (reader-aligned, tools walked through).
5. **The hard cases** — diagnosed-but-won't-move; the unfixable lowest layer.
6. **Belief, then Faith** — the volition finale (Decision C).
7. **Watching it change + The Growth Map** — pays off the Doorway.
8. **Appendix** — Troubleshooting Reference + any grids.

## Human prerequisites (only the author can supply)
- **The author's note** (Introduction) — must be true; cannot be fabricated.
- Sign-off on **"What this book is not"** (clinical-boundary frontmatter — drafted for approval).
