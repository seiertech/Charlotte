# Character Bible — THE SYSTEM OF YOU

> **Single source of truth for all named characters in the Draft 4 manuscript.** The rewrite pipeline uses ONLY these characters. No agent may introduce a new named character. The cast is closed.

---

## Cast

### Maren

- **Age:** 34
- **Occupation:** Night-shift charge nurse (A&E department)
- **Situation:** Two years into night shifts; a single parent to a five-year-old. Functioning on fragmented sleep, her life built around other people's emergencies with no space for her own recovery. Originally from a small town in Denmark, now living in East London.
- **Primary layer:** Body
- **Secondary layer:** —
- **Physical signature:** A blur behind her eyes — the soft unfocus that arrives before she's aware she's tired, as though the world has slipped one frame behind her gaze. *(Body register: blur)*
- **What they carry:** The quiet fear that running on empty has become her normal and she no longer knows what rested feels like.

---

### Callum

- **Age:** 22
- **Occupation:** Final-year architecture student
- **Situation:** Bright, fast-thinking, prone to flash reactions he can't explain. Grew up in a loud household in Glasgow where you spoke first or you weren't heard. Now in a relationship where his speed keeps causing damage.
- **Primary layer:** Wiring
- **Secondary layer:** Room
- **Physical signature:** A gear-slip in his voice — a half-second where his mouth fires before his thinking catches up, the words already loose before he meant them. *(Wiring register: gear-slip, reaction-before-decision)*
- **What they carry:** The shame of hurting people with a speed he didn't choose and doesn't know how to slow.

---

### Grace

- **Age:** 47
- **Occupation:** Regional sales manager for a building-supplies company
- **Situation:** Fifteen years in the same role, running the same routes, saying the same phrases. Efficient, reliable, invisible. Recently passed over for promotion and can't understand why — she does everything right, automatically, and that's the problem. Lives alone in a tidy semi-detached in the Midlands.
- **Primary layer:** Habit
- **Secondary layer:** Story
- **Physical signature:** A groove in her routine — the autopilot quality of her hands reaching for keys, phone, bag in the same order every morning, her body moving through the day like a track already worn into the floor. *(Habit register: groove, automaticity)*
- **What they carry:** The suspicion that she has sleepwalked through her own life and might not know how to do it any other way.

---

### Marcus

- **Age:** 58
- **Occupation:** Semi-retired carpenter; now volunteering two days a week at a men's shed
- **Situation:** Widowed eighteen months ago. His adult children live in other cities. The house is quiet. He's surrounded by people at the shed but hasn't told a single one how he's actually doing. A practical man from the Welsh borders who built things his whole life and now finds himself in a house he can't fix.
- **Primary layer:** Room
- **Secondary layer:** Body
- **Physical signature:** A weather change in the room when he enters — the slight drop in temperature everyone senses but nobody names, the pulled-back quiet that makes people speak more gently around him. *(Room register: temperature, weather)*
- **What they carry:** The grief he won't burden anyone with, and the slow discovery that silence is not the same as strength.

---

### Elena

- **Age:** 40
- **Occupation:** Deputy headteacher at a secondary school
- **Situation:** High-achieving, respected, exhausted by a story she wrote for herself at seventeen: that she must be the one who holds everything together. When anything loosens, an uninvited sentence arrives — *you're about to be found out.* The success is real. The story beneath it hasn't updated in twenty years. Her Italian grandmother's voice still echoes in expectations she can't shake.
- **Primary layer:** Story
- **Secondary layer:** Habit
- **Physical signature:** An uninvited sentence — the sudden, silent line of self-narration that appears behind her eyes a beat before she speaks, as if someone else is writing her captions. *(Story register: narration, the uninvited sentence)*
- **What they carry:** The gap between who she appears to be and who the inner voice insists she really is — and the quiet exhaustion of maintaining both.

## Usage Rules (for all pipeline agents)

1. **Cast is closed.** No agent may introduce a new named character not listed above.
2. **Replacement mapping.** When a Draft 3 chapter uses a character not in this Bible, the Rewrite Agent SHALL replace that character with the Bible character assigned to the same primary layer, preserving narrative function.
3. **Unnamed figures.** Unnamed background figures (e.g., "the colleague," "a student") are permitted for incidental interaction — maximum 2 per chapter, no dialogue exceeding one sentence.
4. **Physical signatures are mandatory.** Each character's signature detail MUST appear on every appearance, however briefly — this is how the reader recognises them without re-introduction.
5. **The Doorway (Ch01).** All five characters appear together for the five-cause proof: one shared symptom (the 3 p.m. snap), five different causes, five different bodies. This is the cast's debut and the taxonomy's demonstration.
6. **The Growth Map (Ch11).** All five characters reappear to show their arcs resolving — the book's ending pays off its first page.
